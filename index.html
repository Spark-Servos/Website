// 01 · who we are

Powered by sparks, steered by servos

We are Spark Servos, a brand new team in California competing in FIRST Tech Challenge (FTC). Seven students from 7th and 8th grade design, build and program a robot that has to think for itself in autonomous and then drive fast in the driver-controlled period.

BUILD

Metal, motors and gears. We design a robot, test it, break it and make it stronger.

CODE

Programs that drive the robot on its own and give drivers smooth, precise control.

TEAMWORK

Seven teammates, one goal: learn fast, help each other and have a blast doing it.
